About

Dedicated and passionate professional with demonstrated expertise in teaching, guidance, and counselling. Adept at explaining complex engineering concepts and practices in a clear and accessible manner. A strong team player with the ability to resolve conflicts professionally.

  • Fourteen years of teaching experience.
  • More than six years of experience in the IT industry.
  • Extensive experience in designing and developing real-world desktop, web, Android and iOS applications.
  • Worked on several interdisciplinary projects along with researchers of Kasturba Medical College (Manipal & Mangalore), Manipal College of Nursing and Manipal College of Health Professions, Manipal.
  • Partner at Aironc Healthcare Pvt. Ltd, contributing strategic vision and leadership to drive growth and achieve organizational objectives.
  • Inspired, guided, and mentored students as a Faculty Advisor.
